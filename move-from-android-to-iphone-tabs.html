ANDROID TO IPHONE WORKFLOW GUIDE

Moving from Android to iPhone? Keep your browser workflow.

A phone migration can move important personal data, but it does not automatically make an Android browser and iPhone Safari behave like one shared workspace.

Important distinction: TabSyncBridge does not transfer Android browser history, bookmarks, passwords, or app data. Use Apple's official migration instructions for device data. TabSyncBridge is for the next step: keeping iPhone Safari and a desktop Chrome, Edge, or compatible Chromium browser connected on your own Wi-Fi.

What this helps with after the move

  • See selected desktop browser tab titles from your iPhone.
  • Keep useful tabs available when your phone uses Safari and your computer uses Chrome or Edge.
  • Send a selected close request to the browser profile that owns the tab.
  • Pair your own devices on the same private local network without creating a cloud account for TabSyncBridge.

What it does not claim to do

  • It is not a replacement for Apple's device-migration process.
  • It does not import Android Chrome history, bookmarks, passwords, or open tabs.
  • It does not copy browser data between unrelated accounts or devices.
  • It does not guarantee that every Android app, browser setting, or website session will move to iPhone.

The practical sequence

  1. Complete your phone move using the official Apple process or your preferred manual method.
  2. Install TabSyncBridge on the iPhone if you want Safari-to-desktop tab visibility.
  3. Add the Safari support and the Chromium browser companion for the computer you use.
  4. Put the iPhone and computer on the same private Wi-Fi, then pair them in the app.
  5. Use the linked tab list for the workflow that remains split between iPhone Safari and desktop browsing.

Is this the right fit?

It is a fit if you are moving to iPhone but still use Chrome or Edge on a Mac or PC, and you want a small private bridge rather than moving your whole desktop workflow into Safari. If you only use Apple devices, native Apple tab sharing may be simpler. If you want a full browser-data migration, use the official migration and browser import tools instead.
